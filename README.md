🚀 DasArtikel - German Noun Gender Training
An interactive web application designed to help learners master German noun genders (der, die, das) through a focused and gamified experience.

🕹️ Game Modes
Standard Mode: The classic experience. Practice with a curated selection of nouns to build your core German vocabulary.

Zen Mode: Practice at your own pace. This mode is perfect for a relaxed session where you can focus on learning without any pressure or timers.

Time Attack: Test your speed and precision. Challenge yourself to see how many articles you can correctly identify before the clock runs out.

✨ Key Features
Weekly Word Lists: Content refreshes every Monday with new vocabulary to ensure a consistent and evolving learning journey.

Master the Genders: Specifically designed to tackle the challenge of German noun genders in a balanced and effective way.

Clean Design: A minimalist and distraction-free environment for serious learners who want to focus on their progress.
